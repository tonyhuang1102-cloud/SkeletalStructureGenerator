CARBO’S LITTLE FIELD GUIDE

Small symbols. Big ideas.

SMILES

A compact way to describe connectivity. Try CCO for ethanol or c1ccccc1 for benzene. Letter case matters.

Condensed formulas

Use connected groups such as CH3CH2OH or CH3CH(CH3)OH. A molecular formula like C2H6O alone cannot identify one structure.

Hybridization

sp³, sp², and sp describe local bonding. These assignments are a useful chemical model, not a measurement of geometry.
